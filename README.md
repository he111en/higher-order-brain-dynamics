# Moving Beyond Pairwise Interactions
### Investigating Higher-Order Interactions of Bistable Perception with Dynamic O-Information

**Computational Neuroscience · 7T fMRI · Dynamical Systems · Information Theory**

## Overview

This project investigates higher-order interactions in brain activity during bistable visual perception, building on the experimental paradigm and findings of Pizzuti et al. (2025), *In the brain of the beholder: bi-stable motion reveals mesoscopic-scale feedback modulation in V1*.

The original study used ultra-high-field 7T fMRI to investigate feedback-related modulation between primary visual cortex (V1) and motion-sensitive visual area hMT+ during ambiguous and physically alternating motion.

Building on this work, the present project examines whether interactions involving multiple cortical regions can reveal aspects of perceptual brain dynamics that are not captured by pairwise measures alone. Utilizing dynamic 0-information algorithm, to quanitfy higher=order dependincies in the brain, so asto investigate redundancy and/or synargy in activation contribution and interactions concerning bistable perception, and specifically the perceptual switch.

The central objective is to assess whether information-theoretic measures provide complementary insights into the collective dynamics of perceptual brain networks.
